Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining.

Do I need previous track & field experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. Coaches will help you learn the fundamentals and find events that suit you.

Do I have to compete?

Competition is encouraged, but speak with your coach about your individual goals and circumstances.

What should I bring to training?

Bring suitable sports clothing, running shoes, water, and anything your coach has specifically requested.

How will I know about competitions?

Competition dates will be posted on this page and communicated through the school's normal team announcements.

Can I participate in multiple events?

Yes. Athletes can normally participate in multiple events, although the final competition schedule and event limits depend on the meet.

Who can I contact if I have questions?

Speak to the school's Track & Field coach or athletics coordinator for questions about training, eligibility, competitions, or team selection.
